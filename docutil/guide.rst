######################
Climbing in Montenegro
######################

.. background:: ./background.avif

.. note::
    This guide is created by `climbers-heaven <https://www.climbers-heaven.me>`__. If you want to support
    us in maintenance this guide, book you apartments `here <https://www.climbers-heaven.me>`__.

It's hard to believe that Montenegro is still an insider tip when it comes to climbing.
It offers hundreds of routes on first-class rock, is easy to reach by plane and has an excellent wheat beer.

When you arrive in Podgorica airport you can often visit the first climbing area on the day of arrival!

However, we are pleased to present the first fully interactive climbing guide for Montenegro.

Here are a few introductory chapters:

* `The Climbing Areas`_: An overview of the climbing areas of Montenegro.
* `How to use this guide`_: Explains the features of the guide.
* `Behavior on the rock`_: In short: just use your common sense.
* `Seasons`_: When to climb where in Montenegro.
* `Where to go if`_: For people with special needs.


*********************
How to use this guide
*********************

The guide is programmed as a PWA (Progressive Web App) and optimized for the phone. You can 
`install this guide <https://medium.com/progressivewebapps/how-to-install-a-pwa-to-your-device-68a8d37fadc1>`__
and use it offline.

All images, maps, topos can be enlarged with a double click. In this mode you can also zoom the images as desired.
You can go back with another double click.

The current map or topo can also be zoomed, if you turn the phone 90 degrees.

To avoid getting lost you can activate a gps tracker that shows your position on the map.

The routes can be clicked in the topos, but in unzoomed mode this can be a bit fiddly with climbers' fingers.

If you want to remember a route, click on it and set a bookmark in your browser.

If any symbol is unclear, click on it and you will find help.

In the Menu the option "Move to current Sector" will show you the sector next to your current gps location.

The Srdjan grading is a local grading system with only 4 grades. For many climbers from abroad it is confusing because it looks like
French grading. Meanwhile the real French grading has been established and this is what we only use in this guide. 

Sometimes you can still see Srdjan grading on the rock, so don't get confused.


********************
Behavior on the rock
********************

Treat the rock like a stranger's toilet... Leave it and the rock as you found it, or cleaner!

In short:

* It doesn't hurt your karma to take not only your own rubbish with you.
* Treat the locals and your fellow climbers with respect and kindness.
* If you have to come to the rock with a subwoofer, ask your fellow climbers if your music bothers them.
* If your dog has to bark at everyone who walks past, don't put it in the middle of the path.
* In most areas, the number of parking spaces is limited, so park in a way that others can also have space.
  Five cars for five climbers is not particularly social towards others.
* Last not least: Climbing is a potentially dangerous sport. Always act in such a way that you do not endanger yourself or others.
 
Simple, right?


*******
Seasons
*******

Montenegro is actually a year-round climbing area. With a breeze you can still climb well in the mornings in `Smokovac`_
even in August. Nevertheless, higher altitudes are certainly more pleasant.

* November to April: This is definitely the best time for sport climbers. Even in January you can usually climb in a T-shirt. 
  The best climbing areas are `Lovka`_, `Cijevna Lower Wall`_, `Stari Bar`_. `Wonderwall`_ is also a good option, but unfortunately 
  an unpleasant cold north wind can sometimes blow through there. `Smokovac`_ is in the shade for a long time and is usually wet. 
  The higher areas are all cold or wet.

* April to July: `Lovka`_, `Cijevna Lower Wall`_ and can still be good. `Smokovac`_ is the top area.
  
* July to September: Best Condition in the Mountains: `Žabljak`_, `Kolašin`_, `Berane`_, `Gusinje`_, but also `Smokovac`_ can have a cold breeze and is
  ok until 2pm.

 * September to November: First look on weather forecast! It can rain a lot for weeks. But once the weather beomes stable
   climbing conditions are perfect in all areas in `Podgorica`_, `Cijevna Canyon`_, `Bar`_, `Kotor`_.
 

**************
Where to go if
**************

* You have children: `Smokovac`_,  `Lovka`_, `Cijevna Lower Wall`_, `Žabljak`_
* Is is raining: `Smokovac`_, `Stari Bar`_, `Wonderwall`_, `Kotor`_
* It is hot: `Žabljak`_,  `Kolašin`_, `Berane`_, `Gusinje`, `Smokovac`_ in the morning.
* It is cold: `Lovka`_, `Cijevna Lower Wall`_
