######################
Climbing in Montenegro
######################

.. background:: ./background.avif

.. note::

Montenegro is still something of an insider tip when it comes to climbing.
It offers hundreds of routes on excellent rock, is easy to reach by plane, and is known for its excellent wheat beer.

When you arrive at Podgorica Airport, you can often reach your first climbing area on the same day!

However, we are pleased to present the first fully interactive climbing guide for Montenegro.

Here are a few introductory chapters:

* `The Climbing Areas`_: An overview of the climbing areas of Montenegro.
* `How to use this guide`_: Explains the features of the guide.
* `Behavior on the rock`_: In short: just use your common sense.
* `Seasons`_: When to climb where in Montenegro.
* `Where to go if`_: For people with special needs.

********************
Behavior on the rock
********************

Treat the rock like a stranger's toilet: leave it as you found it, or even cleaner!

In short:

* Do not chip holds! There are enough routes for all levels, you don't have to destroy the route for your ego. Let others climb it.
* It does not hurt your karma to take more than just your own rubbish with you.
* Treat the locals and your fellow climbers with respect and kindness.
* If you have to come to the rock with a subwoofer, ask your fellow climbers whether your music bothers them.
* If your dog has to bark at everyone who walks past, do not put it in the middle of the path.
* In most areas, the number of parking spaces is limited, so park in a way that leaves space for others.
  Five cars for five climbers is not particularly considerate.
* Last but not least: Climbing is a potentially dangerous sport. Always act in such a way that you do not endanger yourself or others.

Simple, right?


*********************
How to use this guide
*********************

The guide is built as a PWA (Progressive Web App) and optimized for mobile use. You can
`install this guide <https://medium.com/progressivewebapps/how-to-install-a-pwa-to-your-device-68a8d37fadc1>`__
and use it offline.

All images, maps, and topos can be enlarged with a double tap. In this view, you can also zoom the images as needed.
A second double tap takes you back.

The current map or topo can also be zoomed by turning the phone 90 degrees.

To avoid getting lost, you can activate a GPS tracker that shows your position on the map.

Routes can be tapped in the topos, but in the default view this can be a bit fiddly with climbers' fingers.

If you want to remember a route, tap it and set a bookmark in your browser.

If any symbol is unclear, tap it and you will find help.

In the menu, the option "Move to current Sector" will take you to the sector nearest your current GPS location.

The Srdjan grading system is a local scale with only four grades. For many climbers from abroad, it can be confusing because it resembles the French grading system. In this guide, however, we use the established French grading system.

You may still encounter Srdjan grading on the rock, so do not be confused by it.


*******
Seasons
*******

Montenegro is a year-round climbing area. Even in August, a breeze can make the mornings in `Smokovac`_ pleasant. Nevertheless, higher altitudes are generally more comfortable.

* November to April: This is definitely the best time for sport climbers. Even in January, you can usually climb in a T-shirt.
  The best climbing areas are `Lovka`_, `Cijevna Lower Wall`_, and `Stari Bar`_. `Wonderwall`_ is also a good option, although an unpleasant cold north wind can sometimes blow through there. `Smokovac`_ stays in the shade for a long time and is usually wet.
  The higher areas are generally cold or wet.

* April to July: `Lovka`_ and `Cijevna Lower Wall`_ can still be good. `Smokovac`_ is the top area.

* July to September: Best conditions in the mountains: `Žabljak`_, `Kolašin`_, `Berane`_, and `Gusinje`_. `Smokovac`_ can also have a cold breeze and is fine until about 2 p.m.

* September to November: First check the weather forecast! It can rain for weeks. But once the weather becomes stable, climbing conditions are perfect in all areas of `Podgorica`_, `Cijevna Canyon`_, `Bar`_, and `Kotor`_.
 

**************
Where to go if
**************

* You have children: `Smokovac`_, `Lovka`_, `Cijevna Lower Wall`_, and `Žabljak`_
* It is raining: `Smokovac`_, `Stari Bar`_, `Wonderwall`_, and `Kotor`_
* It is hot: `Žabljak`_, `Kolašin`_, `Berane`_, `Gusinje`_, and `Smokovac`_ in the morning.
* It is cold: `Lovka`_ and `Cijevna Lower Wall`_
